Headcounts and costs are computed at full site capacity: heads = capacity × drivers/veh ÷ ratio (fractional, utilization and deployed count ignored) — e.g. 200 capacity × 2.8 drivers/veh ÷ 1,000 = 0.56 supervisors. Local OH is a fixed cost: it stays the same whether or not the operation actually fills the site. Ratios can be entered per driver or per vehicle — the two stay in sync through the FTE-drivers-per-vehicle lever (per-vehicle X = per-driver X ÷ drivers/veh). Enter pay as an hourly rate (weekly = × 40) or a weekly figure — they stay in sync. Weekly pay is gross wages; the “Local OH Taxes & Benefits” % in the P&L is added on top. Roles without a pay figure contribute $0 until you enter their pay. Untick a role to exclude it from this composition; “+ add role” creates a custom line in any department. Each line has its own OT % — OT $ = OT% × regular hours (heads × 40) × 1.5 × hourly wage, included in that line’s weekly cost. All heads and $ figures are rounded up to 2 decimals, so the displayed numbers multiply and add exactly. The total flows into the P&L as “Local OH Gross Payroll”.

Fleet Unit Economics

About this dashboard

This dashboard is a modeling tool developed to quickly compare various business scenarios and observe how the bottom line is affected by the numerous cost inputs.

It models the weekly unit economics of one vehicle-fleet site: you set the site's capacity, deployment, utilization and driver shift design, edit every cost rate in a live P&L, and watch revenue, cost, margin, and incremental economics update instantly. Compositions can be saved as named models (shared with everyone who has the password, organized into groups), stress-tested with what-if levers that never touch the saved data, and compared side by side — up to five at once — on the same charts and metrics.

Beyond the weekly view, each model carries a 3-year NPV (term, discount rate, and annual price-change assumptions are all adjustable), so scenarios can be judged not just on this week's margin but on the discounted value of the cash flows they produce as wages, insurance, fuel and pricing drift over time.

  • One site, weekly period. Every $ figure is per week for a single site; rates are illustrative defaults until overwritten with actuals.
  • Revenue = vehicles × utilization × revenue-hours-per-vehicle@100% × billed rate ($85/rev-hr default). Utilization = the share of the week's shifts (14 default) actually filled.
  • Driver shift = 8 hrs + OT%×8, split into Online Hours (revenue generating), Acceptable Gap Time (breaks, Fleetio, deadhead) and Unacceptable Gap Time (non-productive). Revenue hours come only from the online segment; labor hours cover the full shift; OT hours are paid at 1.5×.
  • Local overhead is staffed to FULL capacity — heads = capacity × FTE-drivers-per-vehicle ÷ role ratio, so local OH is a fixed cost that does not shrink when fewer vehicles are deployed. A 22% taxes & benefits load is added on top. All staffing figures are rounded UP to 2 decimals so the displayed numbers multiply and add exactly.
  • Driver-linked costs (payroll taxes, benefits, workers' comp, health, other) are % of driver labor cost, so they move with wage and hours. Recruiting = FTE × weekly churn % × $ per hire. Usage costs = $/mile × miles, where miles = revenue hours × mi/hr.
  • NPV: each future week's cash flow is that week's net profit, recomputed with the key rates escalated at their annual % change (compounded weekly), then discounted weekly at the annual discount rate (12% default). Escalator defaults reflect ~10-year US trends (e.g. commercial auto insurance +8%/yr, wages +4%/yr). No initial investment is modeled.
  • Shared models: saved instances live in a private shared store (100 max), visible to everyone with the password; the last save wins. Deleting requires a separate delete password.
  • Header: live topline metrics for the loaded model — revenue hours, revenue, cost, profit, margin, incremental margin per new vehicle (each hypothetical next vehicle's contribution to whole-site margin, not capped by capacity), profit per online hour, and NPV.
  • Pinned charts (bubbles switch views): sensitivity $/rev-hr as the site fills · where each billed hour goes · profit waterfall · ±10% tornado · net revenue vs vehicles dispatched. The strip can be collapsed.
  • Weekly P&L tab (default): every line item editable — type a rate, scrub the slider, or press-and-hold the +/− steppers; “+ add line” creates custom items in any section. Columns show weekly $, $/revenue-hour and % of revenue.
  • Site levers tab: capacity, vehicles, utilization, miles per rev-hour, billed rate, FTE drivers per vehicle, and the draggable shift designer.
  • Local OH staffing tab: the full roster with per-driver or per-vehicle ratios, hourly/weekly pay, per-line OT %, and custom roles.
  • NPV Calculation tab: term, discount rate, and annual price-change levers with today's cited value and each year's average; cash flows by year with the discounted total.
  • Saving: the 💾 icon saves the composition as a named model (optionally in a group). Loading a model from the ☰ list turns on auto-save until you hit Detach; with nothing loaded you are on Model Zero, the local default.
  • Enter via the ☰ Models list: check one model to load it, check 2–5 to flip into Compare Mode. The ✎ Live editor row adds your current edits as an extra column.
  • Pinned charts (bubbles): weekly net revenue 0→500 vehicles with a draggable margin scrubber · breakeven ladder with each model's safety margin · $ profit per revenue hour vs vehicles · a billed-hour decomposition per model.
  • Metrics table: NPV first, then weekly revenue/cost/profit, margin, per-rev-hr figures, breakeven vehicles, static cost, local OH, profit per vehicle and FTE drivers — side by side, first row and column pinned while scrolling.
  • What-Ifs table: nudge any lever for any single column (fill, utilization, rates, the full shift designer) — press-and-hold steppers, ⟲ reverts a line to that model's saved value, ⇉ sync copies the left-most column's value to every model, amber marks an active override. Nothing here ever writes back to a saved model. The NPV section at the bottom adjusts each model's term, discount rate and escalators the same way.
  • Full shift = 8 + OT%×8 · rev hrs/veh/wk = shifts × online segment · labor hrs/veh/wk = shifts × full shift
  • Revenue = vehicles × utilization × rev-hrs@100% × $/rev-hr · utilization = % of weekly shifts filled
  • Labor hrs = vehicles × utilization × labor-hrs@100% · FTE = labor hrs ÷ 40 · paid hours = labor hrs × (1 + 0.5 × OT share)
  • Driver labor cost = paid-equivalent hours × wage · payroll taxes / benefits / workers' comp / health = % of driver labor cost
  • Recruiting = FTE × weekly churn % × $ per hire · Miles = revenue hours × mi/hr · usage costs = $/mile × miles
  • Local OH payroll = Σ roles ⌈capacity × drivers-per-vehicle ÷ ratio⌉₂dp × weekly pay + per-line OT (OT% × heads × 40 × 1.5 × hourly wage) · fixed at full capacity · +22% T&B
  • Fill rate = vehicles ÷ capacity · Breakeven = vehicles where weekly profit crosses $0
  • Incr margin per new vehicle = site margin with one more (hypothetical, uncapped) vehicle − current site margin, in pp · Profit per online hour = weekly profit ÷ revenue hours
  • NPV = Σ over weeks of [ net profit with each rate × (1+g)^((w−1)/52) ] ÷ (1+r)^(w/52), for w = 1 … 52 × term
