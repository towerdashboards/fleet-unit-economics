Headcounts are pro-rata: heads = driver base ÷ ratio (fractional), so every role’s cost scales smoothly with the vehicle count — e.g. 178 vehicles × 2.8 drivers/veh ÷ 1,000 = 0.4984 supervisors. Ratios can be entered per driver or per vehicle — the two stay in sync through the FTE-drivers-per-vehicle lever (per-vehicle X = per-driver X ÷ drivers/veh). “Heads @ full capacity” shows the role’s count if the site ran at its full capacity (capacity × drivers/veh ÷ ratio, utilization ignored). Enter pay as an hourly rate (weekly = × 40) or a weekly figure — they stay in sync. Weekly pay is gross wages; the “Local OH Taxes & Benefits” % in the P&L is added on top. Roles without a pay figure contribute $0 until you enter their pay. Untick a role to exclude it from this composition; “+ add role” creates a custom line in any department. Each department has an OT % — dept OT = regular payroll × OT% × 1.5, included in the subtotal. The total flows into the P&L as “Local OH Gross Payroll”.
