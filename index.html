Headcounts and costs are computed at full site capacity: heads = capacity × drivers/veh ÷ ratio (fractional, utilization and deployed count ignored) — e.g. 200 capacity × 2.8 drivers/veh ÷ 1,000 = 0.56 supervisors. Local OH is a fixed cost: it stays the same whether or not the operation actually fills the site. Ratios can be entered per driver or per vehicle — the two stay in sync through the FTE-drivers-per-vehicle lever (per-vehicle X = per-driver X ÷ drivers/veh). Enter pay as an hourly rate (weekly = × 40) or a weekly figure — they stay in sync. Weekly pay is gross wages; the “Local OH Taxes & Benefits” % in the P&L is added on top. Roles without a pay figure contribute $0 until you enter their pay. Untick a role to exclude it from this composition; “+ add role” creates a custom line in any department. Each line has its own OT % — OT $ = OT% × regular hours (heads × 40) × 1.5 × hourly wage, included in that line’s weekly cost. All heads and $ figures are rounded up to 2 decimals, so the displayed numbers multiply and add exactly. The total flows into the P&L as “Local OH Gross Payroll”.

About this dashboard

You are editing one site. Set its capacity, fill, and utilization; every rate is editable. Design the driver shift (8 hrs + OT%, split into online hours / acceptable gap / unacceptable gap time) — it drives revenue and labor hours for the whole model.

Local overhead comes from the Local OH staffing tab — role ratios per driver, by department, staffed to full site capacity.

Save a composition as a named instance to share it with the team (25 max) — pick up to five to compare side by side. Loading an instance turns on auto-save: your edits update that saved instance until you detach. When no instance is loaded you are on Model Zero, the local default composition.
